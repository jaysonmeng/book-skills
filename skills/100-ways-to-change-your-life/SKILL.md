## Philosophy — 7 rules to remember

1. **[Above all, suffer less.]** — Wellness is a tool, not a destination. If a habit makes you miserable, it's not wellness. The goal is to reduce net suffering, not increase it in pursuit of abstract health ideals.
2. **[Run N-of-1 experiments on yourself.]** — What works for most people may not work for you. Run single-variable tests to find YOUR answers. Treat your body as a unique data set.
3. **[Think about your death.]** — Contemplating mortality clarifies what matters. It separates momentary discomfort from the deep pain of an unfulfilled life. Your death can guide how you live today.
4. **[Start with your why, not your how.]** — Most people focus on logistics (when, where, how long) before understanding WHY a habit matters to them. The why creates intrinsic motivation the how never can.
5. **[Your best life is unique to you.]** — It's not your mom's best life or your best friend's. What you eat, do, and think every day should reflect YOUR values, not someone else's expectations.
6. **[Small changes compound into big transformations.]** — One green smoothie led to meditation, which led to walks, which led to a completely rebuilt life. Start impossibly small. Consistency beats intensity.
7. **[Never be the one to say no to yourself.]** — Let the world reject you. Don't pre-reject yourself. Ask for the meeting, pitch the idea, try the thing. Most doors open because someone knocked.

2. **Run N-of-1 experiments.** Personalized testing beats general recommendations every time.
3. **Think about your death.** It clarifies what truly matters and what doesn't.
