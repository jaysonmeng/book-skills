### Morning Optimization (Tips 9-12)

1. **Circ walk** — Go outside and walk for 10-15 minutes within the first hour of waking. Morning sunlight resets your circadian rhythm, increases alertness, and improves sleep quality that night.
2. **Morning ritual** — Not just a routine. A ritual has INTENTION. Light a candle, read something inspiring, write in a journal. The ritual signals to your brain: "This is the start of something meaningful."
3. **Identify your chronotype** — Are you a morning lark or night owl? Design your schedule around your energy peaks, not against them.
4. **Get your phone out of the bedroom** — Don't start your day by consuming input. Start by creating intention.

### The Micro-Workout Principle (Tip 14)

Full workouts are great. But if you can't do a full workout, do a MICRO workout. 1-5 minutes of intense movement (jump squats, burpees, push-ups) produces measurable metabolic benefits. "Micro-workouts for mega-benefits." The best workout is the one you actually do.

### The Rule of Three (Tip 19)

Each day, identify the THREE most important things you need to accomplish. Not 10. Not 5. Three. Write them down. Do them first. Everything else is bonus. This combats the overwhelm of the "always more to do" mentality.

### Making Friends as an Adult (Tips 49-54)

### The Personal Bill of Rights (Tip 39)

Write down what you have a right to. Examples:
- I have a right to say no without explaining myself.
- I have a right to change my mind.
- I have a right to prioritize my wellbeing.
- I have a right to take up space.
- I have a right to be loved as I am.

### The 3 Breath Breathwork (Tip 79)

On the inhale: breathe into your belly, then your ribs, then your upper chest (3-part breath).
On the exhale: release from chest, ribs, belly.
Do 3 rounds. This activates the parasympathetic nervous system in under 60 seconds.

### The Life-Admin Day (Tip 74)

Block 2-4 hours once a month exclusively for life admin: bills, emails, appointments, scheduling, insurance, returns, planning. Doing it all at once prevents the constant low-grade anxiety of "I need to do that thing."

> **Case: The morning green smoothie that rebuilt a life** (My Story): From her bed in London, immobilized by agoraphobia, Liz Moody's first step was one green smoothie every morning. Not a full diet overhaul — one blender, one drink. From that single action, momentum built: meditation, walks, returning to the world. **Key takeaway:** The smallest possible habit is the only habit you need to start. Let compound interest do the rest.

> **Case: The Rule of Three in practice** (Tip 19): Moody describes a overwhelmed executive who started writing his "rule of three" on a Post-it note each morning. After 30 days, he reported not only higher completion rates but lower anxiety — because the three chosen tasks were the ones that actually mattered to him, not just the urgent ones. **Key takeaway:** Limiting to three forces prioritization. It's not about doing less — it's about doing the RIGHT things.
