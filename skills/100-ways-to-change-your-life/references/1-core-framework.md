## The Core Framework of the Book

The book is organized into a simple three-phase arc that repeats across all 17 categories:

### Phase 1: Define (How to Define Your Best Life)

The first three tips apply to EVERYTHING in the book. They are the foundation.

**1. Above all, suffer less.** Wellness is a tool, not a destination. If a habit makes you miserable, it's not wellness. Shift mindset from "I have to" to "this serves me." If the habit increases your net suffering, modify it or drop it entirely.

**2. Run N-of-1 experiments.** Your body is unique. What works for most may not work for you. Before making a change: define your goal, establish a baseline, change one variable at a time, measure the result. Use tech tools (Oura, Levels, WHOOP) or analog tracking (diaries, symptom journals).

**3. Think about your death.** Contemplate your own mortality. Who will be at your funeral? What will they say? What kind of life will they describe? Use this to clarify what matters and what doesn't. This is not morbid — it's life-affirming.

> **Case: Liz Moody's green smoothie** (My Story chapter): After a seizure from malnourishment plus weed, Moody developed agoraphobia. She couldn't leave her bed. Her first step? One green smoothie every morning. Not a full routine. Not a diet overhaul. One smoothie. From that single habit, she built momentum: meditation → walks → re-entering the world. Years later, she summited 14,000-ft mountains and hosts a top podcast. **Key takeaway:** Start impossibly small. One tiny consistent action is more powerful than a grand plan that lasts 2 weeks.

> **Case: Dr. Gottfried's Ikaria tea** (Tip 2): Dr. Sara Gottfried read about Blue Zone centenarians drinking diuretic teas on Ikaria, Greece. Instead of assuming it was good for everyone, she ran an N-of-1: she bought organic teas from the island and started drinking one cup nightly. Her blood pressure dropped 5-10 points — the only change she'd made. **Key takeaway:** Personalized experimentation beats general recommendations. What works for a population may or may not work for you.

### Phase 3: Iterate (Measure and Adjust)

Every tip includes a "Want to Dive Deeper?" section with podcast episodes and expert resources. The expectation is NOT to do all 100 tips — but to find the ones that work for YOUR life and iterate from there.
