## Philosophy — 7 rules to remember

1. **[Character is the foundation of leadership.]** — Skills can be taught. Character must be chosen. In disruptive times, when everything around you is unstable, who you are is the only anchor.
2. **[Disruption reveals character; it doesn't create it.]** — Crisis doesn't make you who you are — it reveals who you already are. Build character in calm times to lead well in stormy times.
3. **[Trust is the currency of leadership.]** — Without trust, you can't lead. Trust is built through consistent virtue over time. It's earned in drops and lost in buckets.
4. **[Servant leadership is the highest form of leadership.]** — True leaders put their people first. When the team knows you care about them as people, not just as employees, they will follow you through anything.
5. **[Hope is a leadership responsibility.]** — In disruptive times, people look to leaders for hope. Not false optimism — grounded hope based on a realistic path forward and the leader's own calm confidence.
6. **[Integrity is what you do when no one is watching.]** — In a world of transparency and social media, everything eventually comes to light. Build your life and leadership around principles you'd be proud to have public.
7. **[Persistence beats talent when talent doesn't persist.]** — Disruptive times require staying power. The leader who keeps going when others quit wins. Ziglar's legacy: "You can get everything in life you want if you help enough other people get what they want."

### Core Framework Quick Reference
- **The Central Thesis:** In disruptive times, leadership is not about strategy, tactics, or charisma — it's about character. The 10 virtues form a moral operating system for leaders.
- **The 10 Virtues:** Integrity, Servanthood, Persistence, Discipline, Faith (in people/purpose), Gratitude, Courage, Humility, Wisdom, Hope.
- **The Ziglar Legacy:** Tom Ziglar, son of Zig Ziglar, carries forward the Ziglar philosophy of character-based success. "Born to Win" is the family motto.
- **Disruption Response Model:** When chaos hits → Return to character (the anchor) → Serve your people (build trust) → Communicate grounded hope (give direction) → Persist (outlast the disruption).

### Key Principles
1. **Leadership is an inside job.** — Before you lead others, you must lead yourself. The virtues are internal disciplines first.
2. **Trust is built through consistent small acts.** — Not one grand gesture, but daily decisions that align with your stated values.
3. **Servant leadership is not weakness.** — It's the highest strength. Serving your team empowers them to serve customers.
4. **Hope without honesty is manipulation.** — Be truthful about the challenge. Then show the path forward.
5. **Your character is your legacy.** — At the end of your leadership journey, what matters is not what you achieved but who you became and who you helped.
6. **Persistence is the differentiator.** — In disruptive times, most people quit. The leader who keeps going — while serving others — wins.
7. **Gratitude transforms culture.** — Leaders who practice and express gratitude create organizations where people want to be.
