## Key Principles

### 1. Character Is Not Born — It's Built
No one is born with integrity, courage, or discipline. These are practiced daily. The virtues are muscles, not gifts.

### 2. Disruption Reveals, Not Creates
A crisis doesn't create your character — it exposes it. If you're selfish in calm times, you'll be selfish in crisis. Build character BEFORE you need it.

### 3. Trust Is the Multiplier
In high-trust organizations, everything is faster and cheaper. In low-trust organizations, everything is slower and more expensive. Trust is built through integrity (keeping promises), competence (doing good work), and care (serving others).

### 4. Servant Leadership Requires Strength
Real strength is not domination — it's service. Putting others first when you're under pressure takes enormous character.

### 5. Hope Must Be Grounded
False optimism destroys trust faster than pessimism. Grounded hope = honest assessment of the challenge + a clear path forward + demonstrated commitment.

### 6. Gratitude Is Strategic
A grateful leader creates a grateful culture. Grateful cultures outperform fearful ones. Gratitude reframes challenges as opportunities.

### 7. Persistence Without Purpose Is Stubbornness
"Why are you persisting?" matters more than "Are you persisting?" Purpose gives persistence meaning.

> **Case: The leader who stayed when everyone cut** (2008 crisis): During the 2008 financial crisis, one financial services CEO refused layoffs while competitors made massive cuts. He implemented temporary salary reductions across all levels including his own. When the economy recovered, his firm had the most loyal workforce in the industry. **Key takeaway:** Servant leadership in crisis builds loyalty that compounds long after.

> **Case: The CEO who admitted he didn't know** (Tom's consulting): A manufacturing CEO facing cash flow crisis wanted to cut employee benefits. Tom advised him to first practice humility — admit to his team he didn't have answers and ask for ideas. The team generated cost-saving ideas that outperformed any top-down cuts. **Key takeaway:** Humility doesn't mean weakness. It means strength enough to ask for help.
