### Trust-Building Protocol
**Three pillars:** 1) Competence — keep learning 2) Integrity — keep promises 3) Care — listen and serve.
**Weekly trust check:** What did I promise this week? Did I deliver? Did I listen more than I spoke?

### The Crisis Communication Framework
1. Acknowledge reality — "Here's what's happening."
2. Name the values — "Here's what hasn't changed."
3. State the path — "Here's what we're doing."
4. Give grounded hope — "Here's why I believe we can get through."
5. Repeat. Often.

### Building a Virtue-Based Culture
- Publicly recognize team members living the virtues (weekly)
- Start meetings with purpose (every meeting)
- Express gratitude to individuals (daily)
- Make decisions based on values, not pressure (as needed)
- Admit mistakes openly (as needed)

### The 7-Day Character Challenge
Spend 7 days focused on ONE virtue. Morning: set intention. Throughout: catch violations. Evening: journal.

> **Case: The virtue that saved a company** (Tom's consulting): A mid-sized manufacturing company was facing a cash flow crisis. The CEO's instinct was to cut employee benefits and tighten financial controls. Tom advised him to first practice the virtue of humility — admit to his team that he didn't have all the answers and ask for their ideas. The team came up with cost-saving and revenue-generating ideas that saved more than any top-down cuts would have. **Key takeaway:** Humility doesn't mean weakness. It means strength enough to ask for help.
