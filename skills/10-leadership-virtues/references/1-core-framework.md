## The 10 Virtues Framework

Tom Ziglar's leadership model is built on 10 character virtues organized around the central challenge of leading through turbulence. The virtues are not a checklist — they are a moral operating system that grows stronger with practice.

### The 10 Virtues

| Virtue | Core Question | Disruption Application |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Integrity | Are you the same person in public and private? | When everything shifts, integrity is the only rock. People trust what they can see is consistent. |
| 2. Servanthood | Whose needs come first? | In disruption, leaders who serve their people earn loyalty that outlasts the crisis. |
| 3. Persistence | Do you keep going when it's hard? | Disruption is a marathon. The leader who won't quit gives everyone else permission to keep going. |
| 4. Discipline | Do you do what you should, when you should? | Chaos requires structure. Your personal discipline becomes the team's stabilizing force. |
| 5. Faith | Do you believe in your people and your purpose? | Not religious faith — faith in the mission, in the team's ability, in the future. |
| 6. Gratitude | Do you recognize and express thanks? | Gratitude is the antidote to fear. A grateful leader creates a resilient culture. |
| 7. Courage | Do you do the right thing even when it's hard? | Disruptive times demand difficult decisions. Courage is acting despite fear. |
| 8. Humility | Do you know your limits? | Strong leaders know they don't have all the answers. Humility opens the door to collaboration. |
| 9. Wisdom | Do you learn and apply? | Disruption is a teacher. Wisdom is what you learn and apply from each crisis. |
| 10. Hope | Do you give people a reason to believe? | The leader's primary job in crisis: provide grounded hope for a better future. |

### The Disruption Response Model

```
Disruption Hits
    ↓
RETURN TO CHARACTER (Integrity, Humility, Courage)
    → What do I stand for that hasn't changed?
    ↓
SERVE YOUR PEOPLE (Servanthood, Gratitude)
    → What do they need right now? Listen first.
    ↓
GROUNDED HOPE (Faith, Hope, Wisdom)
    → Honest assessment + realistic path forward
    ↓
PERSIST (Persistence, Discipline)
    → Outlast the disruption. Keep showing up.
```

### The Ziglar Formula
"You can have everything in life you want if you will just help enough other people get what they want." — Zig Ziglar

This is the core philosophy: success is a byproduct of service. Leadership is not about what you get — it's about what you give.
