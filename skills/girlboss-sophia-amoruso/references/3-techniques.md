# Techniques: Practical Tools from #GIRLBOSS

## The Product Description Formula

Amoruso's secret to selling on eBay:

1. **Tell a story**: don't describe the item — describe the fantasy. Who will wear this? Where will they go? How will they feel?
2. **Use emotional language**: not "vintage blue dress" but "1920s sapphire flapper dress, the kind of dress that makes you feel like you're at Gatsby's party."
3. **Be specific with measurements**: size tags are not enough. List exact measurements. This builds trust.
4. **Invest in photography**: natural light, multiple angles, lifestyle shots. The product photo is your storefront.

> **Case: The $8 dress** (Chapter 3): Amoruso spent more time on the description and photography than on finding the dress itself. That investment transformed an $8 thrift find into a $300 sale.
> **Key takeaway**: The time you invest in presentation determines the price you can charge.

> **Case: The eBay premium store trial** (Chapter 4): Amoruso couldn't afford eBay's premium store design. Instead of giving up, she asked for a free trial. They said yes. She then asked for an extension. They said yes again. By the time they asked her to pay, she could afford it. "The art of the ask" was one of the most valuable skills she learned.
> **Key takeaway**: You'd be surprised how often people say yes if you just ask. The worst answer is no.

## The One-Thing-at-a-Time Growth Strategy

Nasty Gal grew slowly on purpose:

1. **Master one platform** before expanding. eBay was their entire business for years.
2. **Reinvest every dollar** into inventory and improvements. No fancy office, no unnecessary hires.
3. **Only hire when the pain of not hiring is greater than the cost of hiring.**
4. **Follow the customer**: when customers asked for the website, build the website. Not before.

## The Cash Management System

A practical bootstrapper's guide:

- **Pay yourself last**, not first. The business gets paid before you do.
- **Track every dollar** that comes in and goes out. Know your numbers cold.
- **Build a cash cushion** of at least 3 months of expenses before spending on anything non-essential.
- **Use credit cards strategically** — not for lifestyle, but for cash flow. Pay them off every month.
- **Say no to VC money** until you have a clear use for it that generates returns.

## The Art of the Ask Cheat Sheet

| Situation | What to Ask | Likely Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Need a discount from a supplier | "Can you do better on the price?" | Often yes |
| Want a platform feature | "Can I get a trial?" | Frequently approved |
| Want a meeting with someone successful | "Can I buy you coffee for 15 minutes?" | Busy people say yes to honest requests |
| Need customer feedback | "What can I do better?" | Customers appreciate being asked |
| Want a referral | "Do you know anyone who might love this?" | Social proof drives growth |

## The Hustler's Morning Routine

Amoruso's practical habits:

1. **Check sales first** — start with data, not with social media.
2. **Respond to customers** before anything else. Customer service is marketing.
3. **Do the hardest thing first** — the task you least want to do is probably the most important.
4. **Make one improvement** to your product or process every day. Small improvements compound.
